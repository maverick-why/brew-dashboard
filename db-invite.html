Getting Here

Door to tap
in under 2 hours.

No car needed, no complicated transfers. A cross-border coach from Tung Chung drops you at Shenzhen Bay Port, and a short taxi ride brings you here.

Discovery Bay → 時光釀造所 · Step by Step
Discovery Bay → Tung Chung (東涌)
Take the DB Ferry or DB Bus to Tung Chung. Head to Citygate (東薈城), North Gate P3 bus terminus.
~20 min
Tung Chung → Shenzhen Bay Port (深圳灣口岸)
Board a cross-border coach. Two operators run this route daily — buy tickets in advance on the app. The coach crosses Shenzhen Bay Bridge directly, no stops.
~45–60 min ¥40–45 / person
Clear Immigration · Shenzhen Bay Port
Have your Home Return Permit (回鄉證) or passport ready. Shenzhen Bay is generally smooth and fast. Once through, you're in Shenzhen.
~15–30 min
Taxi to Chrono Brewery · 時光釀造所
Take a taxi or Didi from the port exit. Show the driver: 南山智谷产业园 F座 1楼 107
~15 min ~¥20–30

Suggested Weekend Itinerary

A day well
spent.

Leave DB after breakfast, explore Nanshan in the afternoon, and end the evening exactly where you should — at the bar.

09:30
Departure · Tung Chung
Board the Cross-Border Coach

Catch the 09:45 (環島) or 10:00 (永東) from Tung Chung Citygate. Buy the night before to skip the queue. The Shenzhen Bay Bridge crossing is scenic — 36km over the Pearl River estuary.

11:00
Nanshan · History
南頭古城 Nantou Ancient City

One of the oldest walled settlements in the Pearl River Delta, now a lively neighbourhood of restaurants, galleries, and local shops. Great for a wander and an early lunch — try the clay pot rice in the inner lanes.

13:30
Nanshan · Culture
華僑城 OCT Creative Park

Shenzhen's original creative district — sculpture gardens, independent bookshops, art galleries, and some of the city's best coffee. A slow afternoon walk before the brewery opens.

15:30
Nanshan · Shopping
萬象城 MixC Nanshan

Drop in for a coffee or a browse. The scale of Shenzhen malls is genuinely something — MixC alone is the size of several Hong Kong shopping centres. Good for picking up a few things before the evening.

17:00
★ Main Event · 南山智谷
時光釀造所 · Chrono Brewery

We open at 5pm. Start with something from the tap wall — 24 options means there's always something new. Our house IPA 決心 (Resolve) is brewed right here in the back. You can see the fermentation tanks through the glass.

22:00
Return
Head Back to the Border

Shenzhen Bay Port closes at midnight — give yourself time. A Didi from the brewery to the border is about 15 minutes. The coach back to Tung Chung departs from the Hong Kong side.

While You're in Nanshan

Worth the
detour.

Nanshan is one of Shenzhen's most walkable and varied districts. Here's what's around us.

🏯
南頭古城
Nantou Ancient City · ~10 min taxi

A 1,700-year-old walled city turned creative village. Lunch spots, local snacks, and architecture you won't find anywhere else in the city.

🎨
華僑城創意文化園
OCT Creative Park · ~12 min taxi

Old factories converted into galleries, studios, and cafés. One of Shenzhen's best neighbourhoods for an aimless afternoon wander.

🌏
世界之窗 / 錦繡中華
Window of the World · ~15 min

Miniature world landmarks side by side — more fun than it sounds, especially with kids. A uniquely Shenzhen experience.

🛍️
萬象城 MixC
Nanshan · ~8 min taxi

Shenzhen's flagship mall. Luxury brands, a solid food hall, and enough space to get genuinely lost. Good air con on a hot day.

🏙️
萬科雲城
Vanke Cloud City · ~5 min taxi

A newer tech and lifestyle development with good coffee and a more local, less touristy atmosphere than the big malls.

🚢
蛇口漁人碼頭
Shekou Waterfront · ~25 min

Restaurants and bars along the waterfront near Shekou Ferry Terminal. A nice evening walk if you have time before heading to the brewery.
